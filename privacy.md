# Privacy Policy for Guildian

Effective Date: 30/04/2025

Your privacy is important to us. Guildian is committed to handling data responsibly and transparently.

## 1. Data Collection
Guildian only collects the minimum necessary information to function, which may include:
- Discord server (guild) IDs
- Channel IDs for settings
- User IDs for moderation and utility features
- Configuration settings stored per server

We do NOT collect:
- Personal identifiable information (PII)
- Messages or content not directly required by bot features

## 2. Data Usage
- Data is used solely to provide the bot's features (like moderation, events, or custom settings).
- Data is never sold, shared, or used for profiling.

## 3. Data Storage
- All stored data remains within the bot's internal storage or hosting environment.
- If you remove the bot from a server, associated data may be deleted.

## 4. Your Rights
- You may request data removal by contacting the bot owner.
- Admins may reset settings or stored data via commands (where applicable).

## 5. Contact
For privacy concerns, reach out on Discord: `Maiko_1264` or through the (bot's support server)soon.

We may update this policy as the bot grows. Continued use of Guildian implies acceptance of the current version.
